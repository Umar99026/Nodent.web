Privacy Policy

Draft policy for Nodent.

Nodent stores account information, quiz results, comments, chats, written responses, and study progress to operate the platform.

Passwords are stored in hashed form and are not stored in plain text.

Nodent may collect technical data such as request logs for security and reliability purposes.

Users should not post sensitive personal information in public chats or comments.

Moderators and administrators may review reported content to enforce platform rules.

Before a public launch, this draft should be reviewed and adapted for your actual hosting, analytics, and legal requirements.
